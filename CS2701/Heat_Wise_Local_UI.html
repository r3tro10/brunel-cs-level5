    Heat Wise Local

    About and data sources

    Heat Wise Local shows residents how heat risk changes across their neighbourhood, and what to do about it.

    Where our information comes from

    InformationSourceHow often it updates
    Temperature forecastOpen-Meteo weather APIEvery hour
    Heat-Health AlertsUK Health Security Agency, entered by our adminWhen UKHSA issues an alert
    Cool spacesGreater London Authority Cool Spaces and community reportsDaily, reports live
    MapOpenStreetMap contributorsAs the map changes
    Past summersOpen-Meteo historical weatherOnce a year
    Health advice and FAQ answersNHS and UKHSA heat guidanceChecked before each summer

    Accessibility

    We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: large text option, simple view, read aloud, keyboard navigation and colours that differ in lightness as well as hue.

    Important

    Heat Wise Local gives general guidance and is not medical advice. In an emergency call 999. For urgent health advice call NHS 111.
